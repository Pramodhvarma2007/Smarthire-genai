## 2. What skills do I need to become a Machine Learning Engineer?

To become a Machine Learning Engineer, you need the following skills:

* **Core Machine Learning:** Traditional algorithms like linear regression and decision trees using Scikit-Learn [3].
* **Deep Learning:** Artificial neural networks using frameworks like PyTorch or TensorFlow [3].

Job listings also highlight the need for skills like MLOps [4, 5] and cloud platforms such as AWS [6].

## 6. How should I write my resume as a fresher?

**Answer:**
Focus on your potential, practical skills, and eagerness to learn [1]. 

Here is how to write your resume:
* **Highlight Projects:** Explain what you created, the tools you used, and the problems you solved. Use clear action verbs like *built*, *designed*, or *organized* [2].
* **Group Skills:** Divide your skills into categories like technical skills, soft skills, and tools so it is easy to scan [2]. Move the skills a specific job asks for to the top [3].
* **Keep it Single-Page:** Keep content short using standard margins, readable fonts, and simple bullet points [2].
* **Avoid Errors:** Use one simple font and a plain layout. Ask a friend to read it to catch spelling and layout errors [3].
* **Use a Professional Email:** Use an email address with your name [3].

## 8. What is the difference between junior and senior level jobs?

**Answer:**
Junior-level jobs are for people with 1 to 3 years of basic work experience [3]. In a junior role, you complete daily tasks under the guidance of team leaders and managers [3]. 

Senior-level jobs require 5 or more years of experience [2]. Senior professionals make key technical decisions, manage complex projects, focus on long-term goals, mentor teams, and guide overall strategy [2]. They also develop leadership skills, communicate with key business partners, and design overall project plans [2].
