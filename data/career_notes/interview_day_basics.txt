Interview day basics for freshers: what to wear, when to arrive, how to calm nerves, and what to do if you forget a name or a question

**Interview Day Guide for Freshers**

Heading out for your first job interview can feel scary, but simple preparation will help you stay calm and make a great impression. Here is a step-by-step guide to handling your big day.

**Step 1: Wear the Right Clothes**
Choose clean, ironed, and professional attire. A solid collared shirt with formal trousers works well for anyone, or you can pick a simple, neat suit. Keep your footwear clean and comfortable. Avoid strong perfumes, loud jewelry, or overly bright colors. Looking neat will instantly boost your confidence. 

**Step 2: Time Your Arrival**
Aim to reach the venue 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled time. Map out your travel route the night before so you do not get lost or stuck in traffic. Arriving too early might force you to wait too long and build up anxiety, while being late creates a poor first impression. Timing it right shows that you are punctual.

**Step 3: Calm Your Nerves**
It is completely normal to feel anxious while sitting in the waiting room. To steady your mind, take a few slow, deep breaths. Sit up straight, put your phone away, and smile. Remind yourself that the interviewer simply wants to get to know you and your skills, not trick you. 

**Step 4: Handle Mistakes Gracefully**
If you forget the interviewer’s name, do not panic. Simply use polite titles like "Sir" or "Ma'am," or speak directly without using their name at all. 

If your mind goes blank or you forget the question mid-answer, stop and smile calmly. You can say: "Could you please repeat the question?" or "May I have a quick moment to gather my thoughts?" Interviewers appreciate honesty and composure far more than a rushed, messy answer.

**Step 5: End with Confidence**
When the conversation ends, thank the interviewer for their time. Gather your belongings neatly, offer a warm smile and a polite goodbye, and walk out with your head held high.
