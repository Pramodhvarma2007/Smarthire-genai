How to fill in a job application form: required and optional fields, what to write, and mistakes to avoid

# How to Fill Out a Job Application Form

Applying for a job can feel scary, but it gets easy when you break it down into steps. Follow this guide to fill out your application properly and boost your chances of getting hired.

### Step 1: Prepare Your Info
Read the whole form before you start typing. Gather your resume, school history, list of skills, and reference details. Having these ready saves time and prevents mistakes.

### Step 2: Fill Out Required Fields
Required fields usually have a red star (*). These ask for basic details:
* **Contact Details:** Use a professional email address (like your name) and your current phone number.
* **Education:** List your school name, your current grade, or your graduation year.

Make sure your contact information is correct so employers can reach you easily.

### Step 3: Tackle Optional Fields
Optional fields do not stop you from submitting, but filling them out helps you stand out. 
* **Links:** Share your professional social profile or online project work if requested.
* **Extra Questions:** If an optional box asks why you want the role, write a brief response showing your interest. 
* Leave optional fields blank only if they truly do not apply to you.

### Step 4: Write Great Answers
When describing past school projects, volunteer work, or part-time jobs, start with your most recent experience. Use clear action words like *organized*, *created*, or *helped*. Match your skills to what the job asks for. Keep your writing clear, honest, and brief.

### Step 5: Mistakes to Avoid
* **Spelling Errors:** Always proofread. Read your answers out loud or ask a friend to check your work before submitting.
* **Generic Answers:** Avoid writing the exact same answers for every job application. Focus on what makes this specific role special to you.
* **Leaving Blanks:** If a required question does not apply to you, write "N/A" (Not Applicable) instead of leaving it empty.
* **Rushing:** Take your time. Check that any attached files are in the correct format.

### Step 6: Final Review
Check all fields one last time to make sure nothing is missing. Ensure your files are uploaded correctly, then click submit!
