How to Learn a New Skill in 30 Days

A clear plan keeps you from jumping between videos without finishing anything.

Week 1: Learn the basics.
Pick one course or one guide and finish it. Do not start a second one yet. Practice for one to two hours every day.

Week 2: Practice with small exercises.
Solve simple problems on your own. Write what you got wrong and fix it.

Week 3: Build one small project.
Use the skill to make something real, such as a budget sheet, a small website or a data report. A finished small project is better than a half-finished big one.

Week 4: Show your work.
Clean up the project, upload it to GitHub with a short description, and add a line about it to your resume.

Tips:
- Study at the same time each day.
- Use one learning source at a time.
- Keep a notes file of what you learned.
- Ask a friend or a teacher to look at your project and give feedback.
