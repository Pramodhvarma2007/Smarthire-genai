Common Resume Mistakes for Freshers

A resume is often read in less than a minute, so small mistakes can cost you interviews.

1. Too long. Keep it to one page.
2. Vague lines. "Worked on a project" tells the reader nothing. Say what you built, which tools you used and what it did. Example: "Built an expense tracker in Python that saves daily spending to a file."
3. Skills with no proof. If you list a skill, show where you used it, in a project or a course.
4. Mixing tools and personal qualities. Keep tools like Python and SQL in one list. Show teamwork through your project lines instead.
5. No projects. Freshers have little work experience, so projects are your proof. List two or three, each with a one-line result.
6. Spelling and layout errors. Ask a friend to read it. Use one simple font and plain layout.
7. The same resume for every job. Move the skills that a job asks for to the top.
8. An unprofessional email address. Use your name.

Never invent skills, numbers or experience. Interviewers ask about everything on your resume.
