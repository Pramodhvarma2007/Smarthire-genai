How to Choose Your First Tech Role

Many students feel stuck choosing between roles. Use these steps.

Step 1: Think about what you enjoy.
- If you like finding answers in numbers and making charts, look at Data Analyst roles.
- If you like building pages and apps that people can click, look at Web Developer roles.
- If you like teaching computers to learn from examples, look at Machine Learning roles.

Step 2: Read job listings.
Open ten listings for the role and write down the skills that appear most often. Those are your learning list.

Step 3: Try a small project.
Spend one weekend on a tiny project in each role you are considering. Notice which one you want to keep doing.

Step 4: Pick one and go deep.
Spend two to three months on one role instead of learning a little of everything. Employers prefer a few skills shown in real projects.

Step 5: Apply for fresher level jobs.
Look for jobs marked Fresher or Junior. Jobs marked Senior or Lead ask for many years of experience.

Your first role is not your last. Many people change direction after a year or two.
